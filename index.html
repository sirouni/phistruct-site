PhiStruct

Build real geometry with your hands.

PhiStruct is the first mobile app where you actually assemble ball-and-strut geometric models by touch — tap a hole, place a strut — instead of just browsing finished shapes. Every model in the library, and anything you build freely, is guaranteed physically buildable: struts never pass through each other.

Offline, one-time purchase, no account.
