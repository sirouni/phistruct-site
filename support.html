Getting started

Building something

Tap the white ball to see every direction a strut can go from it. Tap one of the glowing holes to place a strut of the currently selected color and length. Keep going from any ball in your build.

Colors & lengths

The four color dots (blue / red / yellow / green) and the three length buttons (1 / 2 / 3) in the toolbar set what the next strut you place will look like — just like a real strut kit.

Model Library

Tap the grid icon to browse built-in models, grouped by shape family and difficulty. Open one for a step-by-step guided build (with a "complete instantly" and "undo one step" option), or toggle a translucent reference ghost and build freely alongside it.

Saving & exporting

Save/Open keep an exact .json project file you can reopen later. Export gives you a high-resolution PNG, a full-color GLB (for Blender/AR/web viewers), or a centered STL for 3D printing — plus a parts list (color × length × count) you can copy to buy or sort real struts.

Why can't I place a strut somewhere?

PhiStruct only lets you place struts that would be physically buildable — a strut is never allowed to pass through another strut. If a direction isn't glowing, that placement would cause a collision.

Report a bug or request a feature

Email hello@phistruct.com with your device model and iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened. Screenshots or screen recordings help a lot.
